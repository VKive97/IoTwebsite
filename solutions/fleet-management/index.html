What Is Fleet Management?

Fleet management is how you organize, administer, and scale the operational side of running a fleet—structuring vehicles, teams, and locations, assigning users and roles, and keeping every part of the operation connected under one platform. It’s distinct from live GPS tracking, which lives in Fleet Tracking, and from Connected Fleet, the product that brings fleet management together with tracking, driver behavior, and maintenance in one system.
