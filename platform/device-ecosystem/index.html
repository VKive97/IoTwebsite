Compatibility, Confirmed

Know what works before rollout.

Device support is verified against the exact manufacturer, model, firmware, connectivity, data fields, and features required for your deployment. A supported manufacturer does not mean every device in that manufacturer’s catalogue is supported.

01

Supported manufacturers

Selected model integrations are available from Teltonika, Ruptela, Queclink, and Jimi IoT. Additional hardware can be assessed for integration.

02

Tested models

Compatibility is maintained at model and firmware level. Ask the Anstel team to verify the current status of your exact device; unverified models are not presented as supported.

03

Device types

Assessments cover GPS and telematics trackers, AI cameras, BLE and temperature sensors, fuel sensors, door sensors, RFID readers, safety hardware, and IoT gateways.

04

Communication and data

Validation may include GPS/GNSS and camera data, BLE accessories, CAN bus or OBD data, and supported MQTT or HTTPS ingestion. Platform integrations are available through REST APIs and webhooks.

Compatibility Assessment

Bring us the exact device details.

Before installation, we review the hardware and the operational data you need, then confirm whether it can be deployed directly, requires configuration or integration work, or needs an alternative device.

  1. 01IdentifyManufacturer, exact model, firmware, network, and region.
  2. 02MapRequired telemetry, events, commands, video, and sensor data.
  3. 03ValidateConnectivity, protocol, data accuracy, and required features.
  4. 04ConfirmDeployment path, limitations, configuration, and next steps.

Compatibility can vary by model, firmware version, mobile network, accessory configuration, and country. Confirmation is required before procurement or deployment.
