Papua New Guinea

GPS tracking and fleet management in Papua New Guinea.

Autonautics by Anstel helps organisations across Papua New Guinea track vehicles, monitor fuel, improve driver safety, protect valuable assets and manage demanding operations.

Industries

Ready for demanding PNG operations.

Transport, logistics, mining, construction, telecom, utilities, security, field service, government and distribution operations.

Regional support

Anstel supports Autonautics deployments and customer operations across Papua New Guinea, including Port Moresby.

Papua New Guinea is an operational coverage area; Anstel does not maintain a physical office in the country. Enquiries are coordinated through Anstel’s Sydney and Suva teams.

sales@anstelglobal.com

FAQ

PNG support.

Will GPS tracking work in remote areas?

Performance depends on the selected device, satellite visibility and mobile network coverage. Anstel can advise on each deployment.

Can one platform manage vehicles and non-powered assets?

Yes. Autonautics can combine compatible vehicle telematics and IoT asset data in one operational environment.
