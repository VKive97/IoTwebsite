Connected Fleet — Manage Every Vehicle from One Platform

Connected Fleet is Autonautics’ fleet management module for running your whole fleet: live tracking, driver management, maintenance, and cost control in one place. It doesn’t just tell you where your vehicles are—it shows you how they’re performing and where you’re losing money.

What Is Connected Fleet

What Is Connected Fleet?

Connected Fleet is Autonautics’ fleet management module. It links your vehicles and drivers to other fixed and mobile assets via smart IoT devices and sensors on a single platform. It then transforms real-time data into meaningful information through predictive, preventive, and prescriptive analytics to support better decision-making and enhance fleet efficiency.

While basic GPS software answers “Where is my vehicle?”, Connected Fleet answers the questions that actually cost you money: which drivers are unsafe, which vehicles are overdue for service, where fuel is being wasted, how to reduce your operational costs, and which routes run late.

How It Works

How Does Connected Fleet Work?

01

Connect Your Devices

Add GPS trackers, AI cameras, and IoT sensors—Connected Fleet supports selected models from Teltonika, Ruptela, BSJ, Queclink, and Jimi IoT, with 20+ compatible device models overall, so you can keep what you already run or install new ones.

02

Get AI-Assisted Insights

Bring real-time vehicle location, speed and status into one platform, with AI-assisted analysis to identify patterns, anticipate issues and support better operational decisions.

03

Automate and Act

Set alert rules, schedule maintenance, and pull reports—the platform handles the monitoring so your team handles the exceptions.

The whole fleet runs from a browser or the iOS and Android apps, with role-based access for unlimited users.
