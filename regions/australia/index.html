GPS Tracking and Fleet Management in Australia

Autonautics by Anstel helps Australian organisations track vehicles in real time, monitor fuel usage, improve driver safety, protect assets and manage fleet operations through one connected GPS tracking and telematics platform.

Connected capabilities for Australian fleets.

Available capabilities include live GPS tracking, geofences, trip replay, fleet management, maintenance, driver behaviour, video telematics, fuel monitoring, alerts, dispatch, reporting and IoT device management.

Industries served: transportation, logistics, field service, construction, mining, waste management, food distribution, utilities and public-sector operations.
