What Is Asset Management?

Manage and monitor valuable business assets with complete visibility into their location, status, utilization, assignments, and operational history from one connected platform.

Asset Management supports both powered assets, such as generators, compressors, and other powered equipment, and non-powered assets, such as trailers, containers, tools, and other movable equipment tracked with battery-powered devices.

Unlike Fleet Tracking, which focuses primarily on vehicle location, movement, and driving activity, Asset Management extends visibility to the broader range of powered and non-powered assets used across an organization.
