Food Services & FMCG

Protect Quality From Storage to Delivery.

Product quality in food services and FMCG depends on conditions that are easy to miss until it's too late. Sensor-based monitoring for freezers, coolers, and storage gives operators a real-time view of temperature-sensitive loads, so they can protect product quality and prevent spoilage across the whole cold chain—not just check on it after the fact.

The Challenge

Spoilage Happens Between the Checks.

Manual temperature logs only capture a moment in time. Without continuous monitoring across storage and transport, an equipment fault or a door left open can spoil product long before anyone notices.

Undetected Excursions

Temperature or equipment faults go unnoticed until a manual check, by which point product may already be compromised.

Storage and Transport Aren't Connected

Cold-chain visibility often stops at the warehouse door, with no continuous view once product is on a delivery vehicle.

Manual Compliance Records

Paper logs and spot checks make it harder to prove food-safety compliance when it's needed.
