Who We Are

Simplifying Mobility Through Real-Time Data and Analytics

Anstel is an Australian technology innovator specialising in enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) platforms, hardware integration, and fleet telematics solutions. Founded in 2010 and headquartered in Sydney, Australia, Anstel develops end-to-end IoT infrastructure and powers Autonautics—an enterprise ecosystem for fleet management, AI-Camera, and last-mile dispatch.

Our mission is to harness real-time vehicle data to save time, reduce costs, and support better decisions across the mobility ecosystem. We help enterprise fleets and commercial partners turn connected data into operational insights and new value. Our vision is a connected vehicle ecosystem where data unlocks smarter decisions for businesses and individuals.

Our Core Expertise

A Full-Stack Innovation Approach.

Anstel bridges the gap between connected hardware and actionable cloud-based insights. Our full-stack approach encompasses three core pillars.

Hardware & Sensor Integration

Native compatibility with OEM CANBus systems, BLE sensors, AI dashcams, fuel sensors, and satellite/cellular GPS gateways.

Device & Fleet Telematics Software

Cloud platform management, dynamic dispatching, geofencing, driver safety behaviour scoring, and automated compliance tracking.

Network and Connectivity

Management of Multi-IMSI SIMs, LEO satellite connectivity, and roaming optimisation, with secure MQTT/HTTPS data ingestion for low-latency transmission.

Global Presence & Telecom Ecosystem

Regional Hubs, Global Reach.

Anstel maintains physical offices and a presence in Sydney, Australia; Gurugram, India; and Suva, Fiji. The company provides IoT capabilities and future-proof solutions and services across Australia, India, Fiji, Papua New Guinea, and New Zealand. In close collaboration with international partners, Anstel’s mobility and telematics solutions serve enterprise customers across Oceania, the Indian Subcontinent, and the GCC countries.
