Dispatch Management

What Is Dispatch Management?

Dispatch management assigns the nearest available driver to the next job based on availability, vehicle capacity, and real-time location—so dispatchers stop manually checking who’s free and where they are. Powered by phone-based GPS tracking, live ETA updates, and dispatch analytics, Autonautics helps improve response times, maximize fleet utilization, and deliver exceptional customer experiences.

Dispatch decides who does the job. Route Optimization decides the best path once it’s assigned—most operations use both together.
