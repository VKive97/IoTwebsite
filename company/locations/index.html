Operating Coverage

Beyond our office locations.

Operational coverage means Anstel supports customers or deployments in the region. It does not imply that Anstel maintains a physical office in every listed market.

Papua New Guinea

Anstel supports Autonautics deployments and customer operations across Papua New Guinea, including Port Moresby. Papua New Guinea is an operational coverage area; Anstel does not maintain a physical office in the country. Enquiries are coordinated through Anstel’s Sydney and Suva teams.

New Zealand

Autonautics is available for New Zealand operations. Customer and deployment support is coordinated through Anstel’s Sydney headquarters and Pacific regional teams; no physical New Zealand office is listed.
