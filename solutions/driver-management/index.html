What Is Driver Management?

Driver management is the use of telematics data to score driving behaviour, track licenses and compliance, and manage every driver’s profile and history from one platform—helping every driver perform with confidence and every fleet operate more efficiently.

Driver Performance

How Does Driver Behaviour Scoring Work?

Every trip generates events—speeding, harsh braking, rapid acceleration, harsh cornering, excessive idling. Autonautics weighs each event by frequency and severity and rolls them into a single score per driver, so you can rank drivers, track trends, and see who needs coaching without reading raw trip logs.
