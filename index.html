The Platform

What Is Autonautics?

Autonautics is Anstel’s cloud platform for running fleets, assets, and IoT devices in one place. It pulls live data from your vehicles and sensors—location, speed, fuel, temperature, driver behaviour—and turns it into alerts, reports, and automated workflows your team can act on. Operators use it to cut fuel and idle costs, keep drivers safe, stay compliant, and deliver on time, without stitching together separate tools for each job.

What Hardware Does Autonautics Work With?

Autonautics works with GPS trackers and telematics hardware from Teltonika, Queclink, and Jimi, AI dash cameras, fuel and temperature sensors, and industrial IoT devices. Keep the hardware you already run or add new devices—there’s no rip-and-replace, and the platform supports AIS-140 and other regional standards.
