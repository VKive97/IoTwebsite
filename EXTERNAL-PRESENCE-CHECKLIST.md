## Profiles to claim and verify

- Google Business Profile: create or verify one listing for each genuine physical office.
- Bing Places: create or verify the same three physical-office listings.
- Relevant Australian, Indian and Fijian business directories: use the canonical identity and matching office details above.
- Technology and device partners: request an Anstel or Autonautics partner-profile page with a link to the relevant product or regional page.
- Customers approving a public reference: request a factual customer story or supplier link; obtain written approval before publication.
- Review platforms: invite genuine customers to submit independent reviews; never create or incentivise misleading reviews.
- Press and industry publications: use a consistent company boilerplate and link to the relevant permanent Knowledge Centre or regional URL.

## Verification before adding a listing to schema

- The listing is publicly accessible without signing in.
- Anstel controls or has verified the listing.
- Company name, address, telephone and website match this file.
- The profile describes the product as “Autonautics by Anstel”.
- The listing links back to `https://www.anstelglobal.com/` or the correct regional page.
- Duplicate or outdated listings have been merged or removed.
