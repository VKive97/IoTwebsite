Built Around Your Life

One Car.
So Many Possibilities.

Built for one vehicle or an entire fleet. The same connected technology can support the journeys you make every day.

01

Individual Vehicle Owners

Stay connected to the car you drive. Check its location, review journeys, and receive configured reminders and security notifications, while the smart algorithm finds the most economical insurance and maintenance provider that suits your budget.

02

Family & Shared Vehicles

Bring visibility to a vehicle used by more than one person. Agree on who can access location and journey information, and which trusted contacts receive supported alerts. Ideal for teenage drivers, keeping them safe for your peace of mind.

03

Stay informed about routes, speed, driving behaviour, and vehicle use when your car is operated by a hired driver or when it is chauffeur driven. Driving fatigue, smoking, and seat belt use are non-negotiable when your family’s safety is at stake.

Connected to What Matters

Your Vehicle.
A Clearer View.

Available capabilities depend on compatible hardware, installation, connectivity and your selected service configuration.

Live Tracking & Trip History

See your car on a live map and review recorded journeys, routes and stops using compatible GPS hardware.

SOS & Safety Alerts

Supported SOS devices can be configured to send speed or driving-behaviour alerts to the people you nominate. Agree on response arrangements during setup; an SOS notification does not, in itself, guarantee emergency-service dispatch.

Geofences & Unauthorised Movement

Receive configured notifications when your car enters or leaves a location, moves unexpectedly, or triggers a supported towing alert.

Maintenance Reminders

Keep everyday ownership organised with service reminders based on the vehicle information and features available in your setup.

Lower Insurance Premiums

Based on your driving patterns and behaviour, we can help you secure lower insurance premiums from multiple insurance providers.

Vehicle Recovery Support

Use available location information and security-event records to support an authorised recovery response. Recovery services and outcomes depend on your selected arrangements.

Mobile Access

Check available vehicle location, journey information and notifications through supported mobile access, wherever connectivity is available.

Supported Remote Immobilisation

Where legally permitted, authorised operators can use compatible hardware and an appropriate service configuration. Confirm supported controls and safe operating conditions with Anstel.

Driver Privacy & Consent

For chauffeur-driven vehicles, vehicle and driver monitoring must comply with applicable notice, consent and privacy requirements. Explain what is monitored, obtain appropriate consent and restrict access to authorised people.
