Who We Are

Simplifying Mobility Through Real-Time Data and Analytics

Anstel Pty Ltd is an Australian technology innovator specializing in enterprise Internet of Things (IoT) platforms, hardware integration, and fleet telematics solutions. Founded in 2010 and headquartered in Sydney, Australia, Anstel develops end-to-end IoT infrastructure and powers Autonautics—an enterprise fleet management, video telematics, and last-mile dispatch ecosystem.

Our mission is to harness real-time vehicle data to save time, reduce costs, and support better decisions across the mobility ecosystem. We help enterprise fleets and commercial partners turn connected data into operational insight and new value. Our vision is a connected vehicle ecosystem where data unlocks smarter decisions for businesses and individuals.

Our Core Expertise

A Full-Stack Innovation Approach.

Anstel bridges the gap between hardware sensors and cloud actionable insights. Our full-stack approach encompasses three core pillars.

Hardware & Sensor Integration

Native compatibility with OEM CANBus systems, BLE sensors, AI dashcams, fuel sensors, and satellite/cellular GPS gateways.

Device & Fleet Telematics Software

Cloud platform management, dynamic dispatching, geofencing, driver safety behavior scoring, and automated compliance tracking.

Network & Connectivity Layer

Multi-IMSI SIM management, roaming optimization, and secure MQTT/HTTPS data ingestion for low-latency transmission.
