Privacy Policy

Effective 26 August 2026

This policy explains how Anstel Pty Ltd collects and handles personal information through anstelglobal.com, including enquiries about Autonautics products and services.

Information we collect

We may collect information you submit through contact or demo forms, including your name, work email, telephone number, organisation, country, product interests and message. We may also receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, referring page and website interaction data through hosting, security and analytics services.

How we use information

  • Respond to sales, support, partnership and employment enquiries.
  • Arrange demonstrations and assess product or deployment requirements.
  • Operate, secure, measure and improve the website.
  • Meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.
  • Send relevant business communications where permitted, with an opt-out where required.

Legal basis and consent

Depending on location and context, we process information with consent, to take requested pre-contractual steps, for legitimate business interests, or to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing and international handling

We may share information with Anstel group operations and service providers supporting hosting, communications, analytics, security and customer management. Because Anstel operates across Australia, India, Fiji, Papua New Guinea and New Zealand, information may be handled across borders with reasonable contractual and security safeguards.

Retention and security

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably required for the purpose collected, business records and legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organisational controls, but no internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where applicable. Rights vary by jurisdiction. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential browser storage and analytics technologies. Browser settings can limit cookies, although some functionality may be affected. We will update this policy if additional tracking or consent controls become necessary.

Third-party links

Third-party websites have their own privacy practices. Anstel is not responsible for their content or handling of information.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to sales@anstelglobal.com or Anstel Pty Ltd, Suite 18, 12 Tryon Road, Lindfield NSW 2070, Australia.

Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the website, services or legal requirements. The effective date above identifies the current version.
