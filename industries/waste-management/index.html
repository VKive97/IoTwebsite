Waste Management

See Every Collection Vehicle. Optimize Every Route.

Waste management runs on routes and collection vehicles moving through the same neighborhoods, day after day. Autonautics enables real-time tracking of routes and collection vehicles, optimizing pick-ups, reducing operational costs, and improving service efficiency—so planners spend less time reacting and more time improving the schedule.

The Challenge

Inefficient Routes Cost More Than Fuel.

Fixed routes that don't reflect real conditions, no live visibility into where collection vehicles are, and reactive dispatch all add up to missed pick-ups, wasted mileage, and higher operating costs.

No Live Visibility

Dispatchers can't see where collection vehicles are or how a route is progressing until a driver calls in.

Fixed Routes, Changing Conditions

Static routes don't adjust for new stops, traffic, or missed pick-ups, wasting fuel and driver time.

Reactive Service Requests

Missed pick-ups and complaints are handled after the fact instead of being caught in real time.
