Connected Waste Operations

Turn Bin Conditions into Collection Work

Smart waste-bin monitoring allows operators to check bin conditions remotely instead of relying only on fixed collection schedules or manual inspections.

Compatible smart-bin sensors monitor fill conditions, high temperature, tilt and battery status. Sensor information is transmitted through supported IoT connectivity to Autonautics, giving operational teams centralised visibility, alerts and collection-workflow support.

Anstel integrates and supports the devices, connectivity and Autonautics platform required for the deployment.

The Challenge

Collection Teams Need Information Before Problems Escalate

Waste collection becomes harder when bin conditions, vehicle locations and collection work remain disconnected. Teams may respond too late, visit locations unnecessarily or discover service issues only after someone reports them.

Identify, Schedule, Collect

From Sensor Reading to Completed Collection

  1. Monitor the Bin

    A compatible smart-bin sensor measures the condition inside the bin and checks temperature, orientation and battery status at configured intervals.

  2. Send Data to Autonautics

    The compatible smart-bin sensor sends information through a supported IoT network to Autonautics.

  3. Alert the Control Room

    Autonautics presents the bin status and generates an alert when a configured condition requires attention.

  4. Locate a Collection Vehicle

    Operators can review live vehicle locations and identify an appropriate truck or field team.

  5. Schedule the Job

    The control-room team can create a collection job, assign it to a driver and provide the required location and task information.

  6. Monitor Completion

    Operators can follow vehicle movement and job status until the collection has been completed or requires further action.

Connected Sensor Data

Monitor the Conditions That Affect Collection Operations

Fill Condition

An ultrasonic sensor measures the distance between the sensor and the waste surface. Autonautics can translate this measurement into a bin condition after the sensor has been configured for the bin.

High Temperature

An internal temperature sensor can generate an alert when the configured temperature threshold is exceeded. This helps the control-room team investigate a potential fire or abnormal heat condition.

Tilt or Fall

A three-axis accelerometer can detect when the bin moves beyond its configured angle. The resulting alert may indicate that the bin has fallen, moved or requires inspection.

Battery and Device Health

Low-battery alerts help teams plan sensor maintenance. Periodic device reports also help confirm that the sensor remains connected and operational.

Optional Location

Selected sensor variants can include GPS. Standard sensors can use the registered installation location assigned to the bin within the platform.
