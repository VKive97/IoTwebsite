Logistics & Supply Chain

Every Delivery, Connected.

Logistics and supply chain operations depend on getting the right driver to the right job, keeping customers informed, and proving delivery when it's done. Autonautics brings IoT-enabled dispatch, tracking, and delivery optimisation together on one connected platform, so nothing falls back on a phone call.

The Challenge

Disconnected Steps Slow Down Every Delivery.

Manual dispatch decisions, no live visibility for customers, and no reliable proof of delivery all create delays, disputes, and support calls that a connected platform removes.

Manual Job Assignment

Dispatchers assign jobs by memory or phone calls instead of matching the nearest available driver automatically.

No Delivery Visibility

Customers call to ask "where is it?" because there's no live ETA or status they can check themselves.

Disputed Deliveries

Without digital proof of delivery, disputes over what was delivered and when are hard to resolve.
