Merchant

What Is Merchant?

Merchant is Autonautics’ marketplace, not a fleet management tool. Third-party providers—insurers, service centres, finance companies, roadside assistance—list their services; fleet managers and vehicle owners discover and connect with them, matched by country availability and fleet data.

Fleet tracking, driver management, and maintenance live in Connected Fleet. Merchant is the separate layer for sourcing outside services like insurance, servicing, and finance.
