Transportation

Connected Fleet Solutions for Transportation Operators.

Transportation businesses run on vehicles moving across cities, regions, and borders—which makes visibility the hardest part of the job. Autonautics brings real-time vehicle tracking, telematics, and route optimisation together on a single platform, so operators always know where every vehicle is, how it's performing, and what needs attention next.

The Challenge

Running Transportation Without Full Visibility Is Expensive.

Vehicles spread across multiple routes and regions, rising fuel and operational costs, driver safety incidents, insurance premiums and changing regional compliance requirements all erode margins when they're managed with phone calls and spreadsheets instead of live data.

Scattered Visibility

No single view of where vehicles are or how drivers are performing across routes and regions.

Rising Fuel & Maintenance Costs

Fuel theft, idle time, and reactive maintenance quietly erode margins on every vehicle.

Shifting Compliance Requirements

Mandatory tachographs and ELDs/logbooks, along with regional standards such as AIS-140, make maintaining audit readiness more challenging than necessary.
