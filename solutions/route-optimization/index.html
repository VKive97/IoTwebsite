Route Optimization

What Is Route Optimization?

Route optimization plans the most efficient sequence of stops for a journey, then tracks the route live against that plan—reducing travel time, improving on-time arrivals, and maximizing fleet efficiency.

It’s the planning layer: Dispatch Management decides which driver gets the job, and Last Mile is what the driver uses to execute the route once it’s planned.
