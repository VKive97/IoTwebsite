Autonautics Merchant

What Is Autonautics Merchant?

Autonautics Merchant is Autonautics’ marketplace, not a fleet management tool. Third-party providers—insurers, service centres, finance companies, roadside assistance—list their services; fleet managers and vehicle owners discover and connect with them, matched by country availability and fleet data.

Fleet tracking, driver management, and maintenance live in Connected Fleet. Autonautics Merchant is the separate layer for sourcing outside services like insurance, servicing, and finance.

Finding the Right Mobility Partner Shouldn’t Be Difficult.

Fleet managers and vehicle owners rely on a wide range of services throughout a vehicle’s lifecycle. Insurance, servicing, finance, roadside assistance, and fleet solutions are often scattered across multiple providers. Autonautics Merchant brings trusted mobility partners together through one connected ecosystem, making relevant services easier to discover.
