Last Mile Delivery

What Is Last Mile Delivery Software?

Autonautics Last Mile is a mobile delivery application that enables dispatchers and drivers to plan, execute, and monitor deliveries with greater visibility and efficiency. From delivery assignments and route navigation to real-time tracking and digital proof of delivery, every delivery is connected through a single platform, helping businesses improve operational efficiency and deliver a better customer experience.

Last Mile focuses on the delivery itself: driver navigation, live tracking, customer ETAs, and proof of delivery. It works alongside Route Optimization, which plans the most efficient routes before a driver leaves, and Dispatch Management, which assigns the next job to the right driver.
