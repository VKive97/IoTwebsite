Incident Detection & Response

Respond Faster. Resolve Incidents with Confidence.

When security incidents occur, every second matters. Detect emergencies immediately, coordinate response actions, and provide operators with the information they need to protect drivers, vehicles, and valuable assets while minimizing operational disruption.

SOS Emergency Response

Receive immediate SOS alerts from drivers and prioritize emergency incidents, enabling security teams to assess the situation and initiate response procedures without delay.

Incident Management

Monitor active security incidents from detection through resolution, ensuring every event is tracked, documented, and managed from a centralised platform.

Real-Time Incident Alerts

Instantly notify operators of critical security events so they can respond quickly to emergencies, unauthorised activity, or unexpected operational risks.

Vehicle Immobilization

When authorised, operators can remotely immobilise compatible vehicles. This can help prevent unauthorised movement and support vehicle recovery during security incidents.

Incident Timeline

View a complete timeline of events leading up to and during an incident, helping operators understand the sequence of activities and make informed decisions.

Operator Coordination

Provide security teams with a centralised workspace to review incidents, monitor vehicle status, and coordinate response activities efficiently.

Incident Documentation

Maintain detailed records of security incidents, actions taken, and response outcomes to support operational reviews, compliance requirements, and future investigations.

Vehicle Protection

Protect Every Vehicle Beyond Traditional Tracking.

Strengthen fleet security with proactive protection measures that help prevent unauthorised vehicle use, secure high-value assets, and maintain operational control. From geofence enforcement to remote vehicle immobilization, Autonautics provides the tools needed to safeguard your fleet.

Remote Vehicle Immobilization

Stop Movement Instantly.

When authorised, operators can remotely immobilise compatible vehicles. This can help prevent further movement and support vehicle recovery during a security incident or unauthorised activity.

Unauthorized Movement Detection

Catch It Before It Escalates.

Identify unexpected vehicle movement outside normal operating conditions, enabling operators to investigate potential security risks before they escalate.

Panic & Emergency Protection

Help, One Tap Away.

Provide drivers with immediate access to SOS functionality, allowing them to quickly notify the security team during emergencies and receive timely assistance.

High-Value Asset Protection

Guard What Matters Most.

Protect vehicles transporting valuable cargo or operating in high-risk environments through continuous monitoring and proactive security controls.

Security Event History

Keep a Complete Record.

Maintain a complete history of security-related events, providing valuable information for investigations, operational reviews, and compliance requirements.

Centralized Security Control

Manage It All in One Place.

Manage security policies, alerts, and vehicle protection activities from a single platform, giving security teams complete operational oversight across the fleet.

Gain complete visibility into fleet security performance through centralised reports and analytics. Monitor incident trends, security events, response activities, and fleet risk indicators to strengthen operational security and make informed decisions.

Security Reports

Access comprehensive reports covering security incidents, SOS events, vehicle immobilization activities, geofence violations, and other security operations to maintain complete visibility across your fleet.

Incident Analytics

Analyze security incidents over time to identify recurring risks, understand incident frequency, and improve operational preparedness.

Response Performance

Review how incidents were handled, helping organisations evaluate response effectiveness and continuously improve security procedures.

Security Event Trends

Track patterns in alerts, SOS activations, unauthorised movements, and other security events to identify high-risk vehicles, locations, or operational behaviours.

Fleet Risk Visibility

Monitor security indicators across the fleet through centralised dashboards that help prioritize vehicles and operations requiring greater attention.

Operational Insights

Transform security data into actionable insights that support policy improvements, operational planning, and long-term fleet protection strategies.
