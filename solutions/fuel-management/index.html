Autonautics fuel management reads consumption data directly from your vehicles, flags theft and waste as they happen, and turns fuel history into reports you can act on—so fuel spend stops being a guess.

Overview

What Is Fuel Management?

Fuel management is the use of connected devices and software to monitor how much fuel a vehicle uses, when it's refuelled, and whether fuel is disappearing somewhere it shouldn’t. Instead of reconciling fuel cards and odometer readings by hand, a connected device reports consumption automatically and the platform does the comparison for you.

For fleets, fuel is often the single largest controllable operating cost—which makes it one of the fastest places to find real savings once you can actually see where it's going.

How It Works

How Does Fuel Monitoring Work?

01

Connect to the Vehicle

An OBD-II device plugs into the vehicle’s diagnostic port and reads fuel-consumption data straight from the engine’s CAN bus—no fuel-tank sensor required on most vehicles.

02

Compare Against the Pattern

Autonautics tracks each vehicle’s normal consumption and refuel pattern, so a drop that doesn’t match a logged refuel event stands out immediately.

03

Alert and Report

Sudden drops trigger an alert to the right person, while every litre is logged into reports you can filter, export, or automate.

Autonautics supports OBD and fuel-sensor hardware from Teltonika, Queclink, and Jimi, plus 20+ other device models—so fuel monitoring runs on the hardware you already have installed for tracking.
