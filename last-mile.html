How It Works

From Order to Delivery.

Autonautics connects every stage of the delivery process so your operations stay efficient, your drivers stay informed, and your customers stay updated.

1Step 1
Order Created

Customer orders are automatically captured from your ERP, eCommerce platform, OMS, or booking system and instantly prepared for dispatch.

2Step 2
Smart Dispatch

The platform automatically assigns the order to the most suitable driver using location, workload, vehicle availability, and route optimisation.

3Step 3
Route Optimisation

Drivers receive optimised delivery routes with live traffic updates, estimated arrival times, and delivery instructions directly on the Driver App.

4Step 4
Live Delivery Tracking

Dispatch teams and customers can monitor deliveries in real time through GPS tracking, live ETAs, and automated delivery notifications.

5Step 5
Proof of Delivery

Drivers capture signatures, OTP verification, delivery photos, and timestamps to complete every order with secure digital proof of delivery.

6Step 6
Delivery Insights

Every completed delivery automatically generates reports and analytics that help improve delivery performance, driver productivity, and customer satisfaction.
