Technology that becomes useful in the real world.

Anstel brings its fleet and IoT expertise together in Autonautics, a connected fleet and IoT platform designed around four practical outcomes: a clear interface, accurate live movement, reliable daily performance and connected operations.

01

Modern fleet tracking UX

A modern, smooth interface helps teams navigate fleet information with less friction.

02

Accurate live vehicle movement

Live position and movement data help teams follow vehicles across routes throughout the day.

03

Reliable everyday performance

Stable mobile access keeps vehicle location and operational status available during everyday work.

04

Connected IoT operations

Connected hardware and software bring tracking data together for more efficient fleet operations.
